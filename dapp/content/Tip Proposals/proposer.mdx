# Proposing a Tip

Tip proposals reward contributors for exceptional work.

## When to Use

- Recognize a specific contribution with evidence (link, tx, or post)
- Request funding from community treasury or a sponsor pool

## Create a Proposal

- Add title, description, and evidence link(s)
- Set amount, token type, and recipient wallet
- Submit for endorser review

## After Submission

- Track approval status and comments
- Once approvals meet the configured threshold, payout is executed on-chain
