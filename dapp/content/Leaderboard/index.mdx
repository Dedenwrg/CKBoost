# Leaderboard

Granted points are aggregated into the leaderboard.

## What It Shows

- Rank, points total, and recent activity
- Filters by time range or campaign (if enabled)

## How Points Are Counted

- Points are minted on-chain when quests are approved
- Bonus points can come from streaks or achievements

## Roadmap

- Time limited events with extra rewards towards top ranks during the event period
