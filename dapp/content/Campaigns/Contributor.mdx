# Contributor

Contributors complete quests and earn rewards/points.

## Quick Steps

- Connect wallet → finish identity verification → join a campaign
- Complete a quest → submit proof → wait for staff review
- Receive rewards/points right away once approved

## Proof Checklist

- Provide a clear link, screenshot, or on-chain tx hash
- Include any required tags/handles specified by the quest

## What You Earn

- CKB/UDT rewards (if configured)
- Points UDT, badges, and leaderboard ranking

## Tips

- Submit proof soon after completion
- Keep verification active to avoid reward lock
