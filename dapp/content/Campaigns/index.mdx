# Campaign

Campaigns bundle multiple quests under one goal (social, community, or on-chain).

## Core Roles

- Sponsor (campaign admin): funds and defines the campaign/quests
- Platform Admin: manages campaigns and assigns staff
- Staff Reviewer: reviews submissions
- Contributor: completes quests and receives rewards/points

## Base Flow

- Sponsor creates a campaign
  - Define quests
  - Fund campaign
  - Set proof requirements
  - Assign staff
- Platform Admin approval
- Contributor completes quests
- Sponsor / Campaign Staff reviews and approves submissions
- Contributor receives rewards/points
