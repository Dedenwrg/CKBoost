# Endorser (Campaign Admin)

Endorsers are authenticated individual or organization that can propose new campaigns.

New campaigns need to be approved by platform admin.

Endorsers can appoint campaign staffs for each campaign to approve submissions.

Endorsers can also propose tip proposals.

Entry to dashboard at [Campaign Admin](../../campaign-admin)

## Create A Campaign

Click [Create Campaign](../../campaign-admin/new) button on top right of the dashboard to create a new campaign.

### Quests Management

Quest is the basic unit of a campaign and the smallest unit of submission, approval and rewards. It is a task that participants can complete to earn rewards. You will need to set up an initial quota for each quest to help me fund the campaign properly.
